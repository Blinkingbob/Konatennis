Tennis Coaching at Kohanaiki

Austin coaches members and their guests at Kohanaiki, the private club community just north of the Kona airport. Private lessons, family sessions, clinics, and tournament preparation for juniors — scheduled around your time at the club.

Coaching for Kohanaiki members

Kohanaiki members get the full range: individual lessons built around your game, shared sessions for couples or friends, and junior coaching that runs from first racquet through competitive play. Austin works directly with members and guests and schedules around your stay — mornings before the heat, or late afternoon when the trades come up.

If you're a part-time resident, a short intensive block each visit keeps your game moving forward between trips. Austin keeps notes between sessions so you pick up where you left off.

Junior tournament prep

For juniors with tournament ambitions, Austin offers a structured development program: periodized training blocks, match play against appropriate opposition, video review, and planning around USTA Hawaii and mainland events. His background building junior pathways in Singapore and Santa Monica — and his years on the ATP tour as a player and hitting partner — mean the standard is set by what actually works at the top of the game.

Guest pros and club events

Austin also hosts occasional guest-pro clinics and seminars at the club, bringing in coaches and former players from his tour network for multi-day events. Ask about upcoming dates, or about putting one together for your group.

Kohanaiki Tennis Lessons — FAQ

I'm a Kohanaiki guest, not a member. Can I book?

Yes — guests of members can book lessons during their stay. Mention who you're visiting when you email.

Can you coach my whole family in one session?

Yes. Family sessions are common at Kohanaiki — two to four players, mixed levels, with the drills adjusted so everyone is working at the right pace.

Do I need to be a member of a club to take a lesson?

No. Lessons can be arranged on public courts, resort courts (for guests), or at your own community's courts. If you have court access, we can use it; if not, Austin will suggest the closest option.

Do you coach pickleball too?

Yes. Austin coaches both tennis and pickleball — private sessions, pairs, and small groups.

How do I book?

Email Actennisinc@gmail.com with your level, goals, and dates. Austin replies the same day with availability and rates.
