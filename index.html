About

Kona's Tennis Coach,
Trained Inside the Pro Game

Austin Karosi grew up between California and Singapore and competed on the ATP circuit from 2009 to 2015, training under Larry Passos, the longtime coach of three-time Roland-Garros champion Gustavo Kuerten. From 2019 to 2024 he worked as a hitting partner on the ATP and WTA circuits, with stops at Grand Slam and Masters 1000 events including Indian Wells, and in 2023–2024 coached an ATP top-200 doubles player on tour.

In between, he spent seven years as head professional and program director at clubs in Santa Monica and Singapore, building everything from first-timer clinics to high-performance junior pathways.

His approach is simple: good tennis is built on fundamentals, footwork, and repetition, and the best coaching makes hard work feel like the best part of your day. He'd rather fix one thing properly than ten things halfway.
