Tennis Lessons in Kona

Austin is based in Kailua-Kona, which makes Kona and Keauhou the easiest places to get regular court time with him — weekly lessons for residents, intensive blocks for visitors, and a junior pathway for kids who want to compete.

Private tennis lessons in Kailua-Kona

Whether you've lived in Kona for thirty years or you're here for a week, the lesson starts the same way: a few minutes of hitting so Austin can see what your game actually does under a little pressure, then one or two things to fix properly rather than ten things to fix halfway.

Sessions run 60 or 90 minutes. For residents, a standing weekly slot is the most effective structure. For visitors, two or three sessions across a stay — the first to diagnose, the rest to drill — leaves you with something you can keep working on at home.

Junior tennis in Kona

Austin spent seven years building junior programs in Santa Monica and Singapore, from first-timers through players competing internationally. In Kona he works with juniors at every stage: beginners learning to rally, high school players, and tournament juniors preparing for USTA Hawaii and UTR events.

For serious juniors, a monthly development block includes periodized training, video review, and a competitive calendar. For younger kids, the goal is simpler — build a love for the game and a foundation that won't need to be rebuilt later.

Where lessons happen

Kona has more court options than people expect. Lessons can take place at public courts in Kailua-Kona and Keauhou, at resort courts for guests of those properties, or at private community courts if you have access. Tell Austin where you're staying or living and he'll suggest the closest sensible option.

Also available: pickleball coaching, doubles-focused clinics for groups of two to four, and hitting sessions for visiting competitive players who need a serious practice partner on the island.

Kona Tennis Lessons — FAQ

Do you teach beginners?

Yes. A large share of Austin's students are adults picking up tennis for the first time or returning after years away. No equipment needed for a first lesson — racquets are available.

Can my kid take lessons if they've never played?

Absolutely. Junior lessons start at around age six, and early sessions are about movement, coordination, and fun — the technical work builds from there.

Do I need to be a member of a club to take a lesson?

No. Lessons can be arranged on public courts, resort courts (for guests), or at your own community's courts. If you have court access, we can use it; if not, Austin will suggest the closest option.

Do you coach pickleball too?

Yes. Austin coaches both tennis and pickleball — private sessions, pairs, and small groups.

How do I book?

Email Actennisinc@gmail.com with your level, goals, and dates. Austin replies the same day with availability and rates.
