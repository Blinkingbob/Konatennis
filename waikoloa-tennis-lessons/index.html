Tennis Lessons in Waikoloa

Waikoloa is about 25 minutes north of Kailua-Kona and home to some of the Big Island's busiest resort courts. Austin coaches both visiting guests at Waikoloa Beach Resort and residents of Waikoloa Village — and he'll meet you at your courts.

Book a Lesson →

Lessons for Waikoloa resort guests

If you're staying at a Waikoloa Beach Resort property, you usually have court access included or available to book. Austin comes to you. The most useful format for a vacation is a two- or three-session block: the first lesson identifies the one or two things holding your game back, the following sessions drill them until they hold up in a rally.

Families often book a mix — a private for one parent, a shared session for the kids, and a doubles clinic for everyone on the last day. Tell Austin who's playing and he'll shape it.

Weekly lessons for Waikoloa Village residents

Waikoloa Village has its own courts and a real tennis community. For residents, a standing weekly lesson — same day, same time — produces far more improvement than occasional sessions, and Austin keeps a set of Waikoloa slots open for that reason.

Juniors in Waikoloa who want to compete can join Austin's development program: structured training blocks, match play, and a tournament calendar across USTA Hawaii events.

Pickleball in Waikoloa

Pickleball has taken over a lot of Waikoloa court time, and Austin coaches it the same way he coaches tennis — footwork, decision-making, and a few technical fundamentals that most players never get shown. Singles, doubles pairs, or groups of four.

Waikoloa Tennis Lessons — FAQ

I'm only in Waikoloa for a few days. Is one lesson worth it?

Yes, if you want something to take home. A single session gives you a clear diagnosis and one or two drills to work on. Two or three sessions is where real change starts.

Do you come to Waikoloa, or do I drive to Kona?

Austin comes to Waikoloa. Most lessons happen at your resort's courts or at Waikoloa Village.

Do I need to be a member of a club to take a lesson?

No. Lessons can be arranged on public courts, resort courts (for guests), or at your own community's courts. If you have court access, we can use it; if not, Austin will suggest the closest option.

Do you coach pickleball too?

Yes. Austin coaches both tennis and pickleball — private sessions, pairs, and small groups.

How do I book?

Email Actennisinc@gmail.com with your level, goals, and dates. Austin replies the same day with availability and rates.
